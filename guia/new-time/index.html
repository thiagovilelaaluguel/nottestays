Chegada

Recepção 24 horas.

Avenida Doutor Antônio Gouveia, 23, Pajuçara, Maceió/AL, CEP 57030-170. O número do apartamento está na sua reserva.

Abrir no mapa ↗

  1. Envie os dados

    Antes da chegada, mande pela conversa da reserva o nome completo e o CPF de todos os hóspedes, e a placa do carro, se vier de carro.

  2. Vá à recepção

    A partir das 15h, a recepção do Condomínio New Time, aberta 24 horas, faz o seu cadastro e entrega o cartão de acesso.

  3. Estacione

    O prédio tem vagas de garagem cobertas e rotativas. Informe a placa com antecedência para liberarmos a garagem.

  4. Suba ao 13º andar

    Use o cartão de acesso no elevador e na porta do apartamento.

Guest guide · New Time

Check-in from 3 p.m. · check-out by 11 a.m. · up to 4 guests

Arrival

New Time building, Avenida Doutor Antônio Gouveia, 23, Pajuçara, Maceió. Before arrival, send the full name and CPF (or passport number) of every guest through the booking chat, plus your car plate if driving. From 3 p.m., the 24-hour reception registers you and gives you the access card. Covered parking, first come, first served. The apartment is on the 13th floor.

House rules

Quiet hours 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. Wear the building ID wristband in common areas. No smoking inside or in common areas; smoking is allowed on the balcony. Induction stove: the right pans are in the apartment. Lost access card: R$ 50. The Wi-Fi password is sent in the booking chat.

Leisure

Rooftop pool 8 a.m. to 7 p.m., gym 7 a.m. to 10 p.m., game room, playground and kids room 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Sauna and hot tub 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., paid separately to the building; the hot tub needs booking. Restaurants in the building: Ruca and Anamá (ground floor) and Toá Rooftop (15th floor).

Check-out

By 11 a.m. Please return the access card at the reception.

Help

Message us in the booking chat or on WhatsApp. Emergencies: ambulance 192, fire 193, police 190.
